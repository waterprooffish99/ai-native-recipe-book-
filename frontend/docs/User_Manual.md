## 1. 🔍 Finding Recipes Instantly (Command+K Search) {#1-finding-recipes-instantly-commandk-search}

Looking for a specific dish or navigation link? You don't need to click through multiple menus.
* **How to open**: Simply press **Cmd + K** (on Mac) or **Ctrl + K** (on Windows/Linux) anywhere on the website.
* **How to use**: Type a recipe name (like *Chicken Sajji*) or a page name (like *Dashboard*). 
* **Keyboard navigation**: Use the **Up and Down Arrow keys** to cycle through the search options, and press **Enter** to select and go to that page. Press **Esc** to close the search panel at any time.

---

## 2. 🍳 Fullscreen Cooking Companion (Interactive Cook Mode) {#2-fullscreen-cooking-companion-interactive-cook-mode}

Ready to cook? Open any recipe and click the **Cook Mode** button to launch a clean, distraction-free environment.
* **Large Visual Steps**: Every instruction is displayed in large, readable text designed to be viewed from across the kitchen counter.
* **Ingredient Checklists**: Check off items as you prep. The steps will highlight exactly what you need.
* **Screen Wake Lock**: Your screen will **never fall asleep** while Cook Mode is active, preventing you from having to touch your device with sticky, wet hands to wake it up.
* **Progress Indicator**: A visual progress bar at the bottom tracks how close you are to completing your masterpiece.
* **Kitchen Guard Alerts**: Highlights safety warnings (e.g. high heat warnings or knife precautions) in red banners.

---

## 3. ⚖️ Scaling Portions (Serving Size Scaler) {#3-scaling-portions-serving-size-scaler}

Cooking for a large family dinner or prep-cooking for one? You don't need to do fraction math in your head.
* **Adjusting Servings**: Look for the **Servings** box on any recipe detail page. Click the **＋** and **－** buttons to adjust portions.
* **Automatic Math**: The quantities for all ingredients automatically recalculate instantly.
* **Easy Reset**: Click the **Reset** button to return the recipe back to its original serving scale.

---

## 4. 📥 Kitchen Printouts (Download PDF & QR Codes) {#4-kitchen-printouts-download-pdf--qr-codes}

If you prefer physical cooking cards, you can print recipe guides immediately.
* **One-Click Download**: Click the **Download PDF** button.
* **Accurate Scaling**: The generated PDF matches your currently selected serving size.
* **Scannable QR Code**: Every printed PDF contains a QR code. Simply scan it with a smartphone camera to instantly reopen the interactive recipe page on your mobile device.

## 5. 🤖 Conversational Cooking Helper (Chef AI Chat) {#5-conversational-cooking-helper-chef-ai-chat}

Need a substitution or cooking advice? Click the **Chef AI** drawer icon at the side of your screen.
* **Substitutions**: Out of buttermilk? Ask *"What can I substitute for buttermilk?"* to instantly receive alternatives (like milk + lemon juice) with exact ratios.
* **Dynamic Help**: You can ask questions about active recipe instructions, food safety rules, or general culinary skills.
* **Response Feedback**: Use the thumbs-up/down icons to rate responses, or the **Flag** icon to report inappropriate or non-Halal output.

---

## 6. 🥦 Using What is in Your Fridge (Fridge Logic) {#6-using-what-is-in-your-fridge-fridge-logic}

Got random ingredients in the fridge that you need to cook before they spoil? Use **Fridge Logic**.
* **Input Inventory**: Open the Chef AI Drawer and type the ingredients you have on hand (e.g., *chicken, rice, bell pepper*).
* **Smart Matching**: The system runs a Jaccard overlap scoring algorithm to find active recipes you can make.
* **Missing Details**: The AI lists exactly which ingredients you already have and which missing items you need to grab from the store.

---

## 7. 🗣️ Hands-Free Voice Controls (Voice Engine) {#7-hands-free-voice-controls-voice-engine}

Avoid getting food residue on your device screen by talking directly to the AI.
* **Voice Search**: Click the **Microphone** icon inside the search input or Chef AI Drawer. Wait for the pulsation feedback, speak your query (e.g. *"Suggest a French dessert"*), and watch it transcribe automatically.
* **Read Aloud (Text-to-Speech)**: Click the **Speaker** icon next to the AI's reply. The Chef will read the answer aloud to you while you focus on cooking.

---

## 8. 🕌 Healthy & Safe Eating (Halal Auditing) {#8-healthy--safe-eating-halal-auditing}

Every recipe and Chef AI suggestion is screened for strict compliance under Islamic dietary guidelines.
* **Blocklist Safeguard**: The system automatically filters pork variants, lard, alcohol, beer, wine vinegars, mirin, sake, and animal gelatins.
* **Substitution Auditing**: If you ask to replace a non-Halal ingredient, the Chef AI automatically recommends Halal-certified substitutes.
* **Reporting Concerns**: Click the **Flag** icon next to any AI response to flag violations or errors.

---

## 9. 📶 Cooking Without Internet (PWA & Offline Mode) {#9-cooking-without-internet-pwa--offline-mode}

Kitchen walls can block Wi-Fi signals. Global Plate is built to survive network dropouts.
* **PWA Caching**: Once loaded, all recipes and layouts are stored locally in your browser's memory cache.
* **State Preservation**: Checking off ingredient checkboxes or progressing steps while offline will **not** lose state.
* **Auto-Sync**: The application caches offline changes and automatically syncs them back to the database as soon as you step back into network range.
