# Global Plate

Welcome to Global Plate, your personalized recipe assistant for global cuisines. This platform provides access to authentic recipes from around the world with personalized guidance based on your background and experience level.

## Features

- **Multilingual Support**: Recipes available in 6 languages (English, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, Persian)
- **Personalized Guidance**: Cooking instructions adapted to your software/hardware background
- **Safety First**: Kitchen Guard safety tips for each recipe
- **Voice Navigation**: Voice-enabled recipe guidance
- **Beginner-Friendly**: All recipes follow a simple 5-step format
